### Why ≤15 min can't hold with ×1.2 (two separate causes)
1. **The math:** a price that grows geometrically in words puts the next tier about
   `elapsed × (step − 1) / step` away once you've bought everything you could. For ×1.2 that's
   about 1/6 of the time played: ~50 min at 5 h, ~200 min at 20 h. A pure KEY-saver bot (no forge
   at all, `k1-sim-keysaver-console.txt`) shows exactly that: realised gaps of 38 → 107 → 201 min
   (casual), growing ×1.2 a tier. Probes at ×1.1 and ×1.05, and at base 10 instead of 40, all
   still land at 20–75 min. **To hold ≤15 min at 20 h the step has to be ≲ ×1.013.**
2. **AUTO-FORGE starves KEY:** `stars.runAutomation` buys a forge whenever one is affordable.
   Forge costs a few minutes, so with that perk the balance never builds up to a KEY tier, and the
   wait settles near "4 forges". That's real game behaviour for anyone with both perks, not a sim
   artifact.

### The shape that would pass (Andy decides)
- Price past T8 = `N × (1 + k/S)` words, LINEAR like the forge, instead of ×1.2. Rough math for the
  casual bot (~28 reference words/min): N = 50, S = 50 → about T225 at 20 h with the next tier ~10 min
  away (strong ~5 min). XP ×2.5 a tier then reaches ~1e160 per letter at 20 h. That's finite and
  formats fine, but it hits Infinity somewhere around 80 h of play, so it would need a softer XP
  step far out.
- AUTO-FORGE should only spend what's above the KEY price, or a fixed share of income, when
  AUTO-KEY is also on.
