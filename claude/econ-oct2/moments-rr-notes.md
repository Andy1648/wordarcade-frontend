# Rebirth Rush moments: feel notes (Sell Lemons, Bee Swarm, Keyboard Escape)

1. Bee Swarm shows every live buff as an icon with its timer in ONE strip at the top of the screen. So OVERDRIVE joins the existing BOOST pill slot instead of getting a new corner.
2. Bee Swarm boosts are short windows (15 min field boosts) that players plan a burst of play around. The timer IS the urgency, so the OVERDRIVE pill leads with the live m:ss.
3. Keyboard Escape rebirth: you hit a level threshold, press the rebirth icon, and speed drops to zero while a permanent multiplier locks in. So our card names the new permanent number (×5 → TOTAL), not just "REBIRTH N".
4. Keyboard Escape spends wins on trails and auras right after a rebirth. Rebuying the cheap early tiers is the dopamine burst, so the card's last line points straight at it: "KEY RESET · REBUY".
5. Sell Lemons Ascend: the empire resets, and the stacked Alien Investor % is the only thing you keep. So the moment shows what you KEPT and gained, never what you lost.
6. Sell Lemons waits until the multiplier offer is big (250–350%) before you rebirth. The payoff number is the hero, and its other copy is small, so each card has one big line and two short lines.
7. Across all three, timed buffs announce with a pop on start and then sit quietly as a pill. That's a one-shot burst (same choreography as FUSE FRENZY), then a pill only; no loops.
8. The one-time "your levels became rebirths" moment works like a Sell Lemons evolution reveal: say the count once (+N REBIRTHS) and the new total, then never again.

Sources: allthings.how Sell Lemons rebirth/evolution guides; techwiser + allthings.how +1 Speed Keyboard Escape rebirth guides; Bee Swarm Simulator wiki (Field Wind, Ability Tokens) + ggwtb boost guide.
