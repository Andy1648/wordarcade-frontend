# MARKS via ROLLS — what we borrowed (Blox Fruits / One Piece fruit rolling)

1. Rarity ladder named like Blox Fruits (Common → Mythical) plus a SECRET top, so the top tier reads like a rumour (esports.net "All Fruits in Blox Fruits").
2. Fruit Battlegrounds publishes its spin odds openly (Mythic 0.11%). We print "1 IN X" on every card and keep the rarer tiers' odds exact (itemlevel.net / commonsensegamer.com).
3. The Blox Fruits Gacha cousin prices a roll by your level. Ours is 60 words at your live rate, so a roll costs the same few minutes of play at every stage.
4. A fruit is about the one you EAT: one MAIN slot. Its multiplier only pays while you wear it, which is the "which fruit do I run" decision.
5. Awakening (Blox Fruits, about 12 fruits) is the "same fruit, but stronger" moment. Dupes become GOLD (bonus ×2) and then RAINBOW (bonus ×5), so a common still has a ceiling worth chasing.
6. Mythicals are game-changers, not just bigger numbers. LEGENDARY and up carry a perk that changes how you play: letters ×2, winner bonus ×2, double rolls, OVERDRIVE every 15 min, FRENZY everywhere, and rebirth keeps 3 KEY tiers.
7. Perks are kept while the mark is owned, like a permanent fruit unlock or mastery. The MAIN slot stays the swap decision; the collection is the long tail.
8. Fruit Battlegrounds' storage-slot pressure became the INDEX: owning more marks pays a small permanent bonus (+0.5% per % collected).
9. Gacha players hate long droughts. Pity guarantees EPIC-or-better within 50 rolls, plus a first EPIC by roll 10 so new players hit a highlight early.
10. Reveal scales with rarity (Sol's RNG / fruit spin cutscenes): commons flip in about 300 ms, epics wobble in the panel, and LEGENDARY+ takes over the MARKS panel full-screen. Every animation is finite and moves only transform/opacity.
