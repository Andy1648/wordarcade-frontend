# JOB 21 — steal from something new (chore/inspo, REPORT ONLY)

Three breakout games TYPE A WORD hasn't mined, each with its single "can't-put-it-down" mechanic, the
concrete TYPE A WORD version, and an honest note on what would NOT transfer.

---

## 1. Balatro — the multiplicative synergy engine ("just one more run")
**The hook:** scoring is `chips × mult`, and you assemble a hand of **Jokers** — passive modifiers that
stack *multiplicatively*. The dopamine is the moment two Jokers combo into an absurd number you didn't
plan. You keep restarting to chase the next broken build. Crucially the escalation is EXPONENTIAL and
*legible* — you can see exactly why the number exploded.

**The TYPE A WORD version — "WORD JOKERS" run mode.** A roguelite run (best fit: a SAT Rush or an
endless Word Bomb variant) where between rounds you pick 1 of 3 **word modifiers** that stack:
"+15 mult per vowel", "×2 for 7+ letter words", "rare words also add their length as mult", "every 3rd
word doubles". Payout becomes `wordValue × (stacked mults)`, and the game already HAS the raw material —
the rarity multiplier, combo multiplier, and lucky ×5 are exactly Balatro-shaped `mult` sources; they're
just applied invisibly today. **Surface them as collectible, combinable modifiers** and the existing
economy becomes a build-a-synergy engine.
**Won't transfer:** Balatro's joy needs *deliberate downtime* to read your board and plan the next buy.
TYPE A WORD's core verb is real-time typing under a clock — you can't "stop and think about your build"
mid-Word-Bomb. The modifier-picking must live in the CALM between rounds (a shop/draft screen), never
during live typing, or it fights the mode's pressure. So this is a new *run structure*, not a bolt-on.

## 2. Vampire Survivors — minimal input, snowballing spectacle + level-up choices
**The hook:** you barely act (movement only; weapons auto-fire), but every level-up you pick 1 of a few
upgrades, and over 15 minutes your trickle of damage snowballs into a screen-filling storm. The pull is
watching a tiny, low-effort input compound into overwhelming power — plus the "which upgrade?" choice
every 20 seconds.
**The TYPE A WORD version — an ESCALATION survival mode.** A single long solo run (5–15 min) where words
keep coming and every level-up you draft a *passive*: "+0.5s per word", "fragments get shorter",
"accepted words auto-chain for bonus", "misses cost less". The screen escalates with your power — more
particles, faster feed, bigger numbers — so a modest typing pace visibly compounds into a spectacle. The
menu already proves the "every keystroke pays, watch the bar fill" feel; this extends it into a
build-over-time run with periodic choices.
**Won't transfer:** VS's entire appeal is that you're NOT doing the hard part — the auto-attack removes
execution so you can enjoy the snowball. TYPE A WORD's identity is that *typing IS the verb*. A mode
where words auto-resolve would dilute the one thing that makes the game itself. The steal is the
**level-up-draft + escalating-spectacle framing**, applied to a mode where you still type every word — not
the auto-play.

## 3. Monkeytype — frictionless restart + a precise skill readout you obsess over
**The hook:** near-zero friction to restart, and a brutally precise, live WPM/accuracy readout with a
per-second graph and personal bests. Typists chase the number for hours because the feedback loop is
*instant, honest, and granular* — you always know exactly how you did and can retry in half a second.
**The TYPE A WORD version — a "SPRINT" leaderboard mode + a real stats readout.** A dedicated timed
sprint (60/120s) with **Tab-to-restart instant retry**, a live WPM + accuracy + *rarity-per-minute*
graph, and a personal-best chase — then plug in the **self-ghost from JOB 13** so you're racing your own
best sprint on the same timeline. TYPE A WORD already tracks WPM live (`wpmLive`) and now has ghosts;
this packages them into the tight, obsessive PB loop Monkeytype nails, adding the game's unique twist:
score by *rarity*, not just speed, so it rewards vocabulary, not only fingers.
**Won't transfer:** Monkeytype's power is ZEN minimalism — a blank page, one metric, no juice. TYPE A
WORD is loud arcade juice (beat flashes, particles, cartoon shouting). Bolting a monospace-calm mode onto
a Newgrounds aesthetic risks feeling like two apps. The steal is the **instant-restart + granular
PB-graph loop**, rendered in the house style (not the minimalism), and aimed at the same
"one-more-attempt" itch.

---

## The one I'd build first
**Balatro's synergy engine (#1)** — because TYPE A WORD *already computes* rarity × combo × lucky
multipliers and hides them. Exposing those as draftable, stacking WORD JOKERS is the lowest-effort, most
on-theme steal: it turns an existing invisible calculation into the single most addictive loop in modern
games, with almost no new economy math — just a draft screen and a legible `×mult` readout.
