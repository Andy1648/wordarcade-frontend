# STEP 19 targets (decided before tuning) — Andy A6

Measured by `claude/progression/econ-sim.mjs` (strict view: no MOMENTUM buys, no purchase under 10 s of
income), three archetypes (10 / 30 / 60 min a day), 30 days + 200 h tail.

| # | target | why |
|---|---|---|
| T1 | median gap between meaningful rewards **1–4 min** below L50, **2–8 min** L50–L300 / whole 200 h | "an unlock or upgrade every 2–10 minutes" |
| T2 | p90 gap **≤ 20 min**, max gap **≤ 60 min** of play inside 200 h | no multi-hour walls (today: 16–22 h) |
| T3 | every number the player sees **< 1e12** inside 200 h; nothing near 2^53 | "upgrades seem insane" = 13-digit prices |
| T4 | a rebirth's climb back to its old level takes **≥ 40 %** of the previous climb | the reset must cost something (today ~5 %) |
| T5 | no sink empty before **50 h** (cosmetics, themes, momentum all still have a next item) | today every sink is empty by minute 11 |
| T6 | CHAIN / FUSE mastery goes far higher (M50) with a **milestone reward every 5 levels** that pays wins | "bars go much higher with better rewards" |
| T7 | regular player reaches L100 in **3–8 h** of play, L200 in **25–70 h**, L300 possible within 200 h | a real late game, not 15 minutes |
| T8 | payout-honesty + menu-no-free-wins + SAT-spam specs stay green | A14 |
