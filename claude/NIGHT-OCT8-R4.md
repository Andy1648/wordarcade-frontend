## Research — how loot reveals build anticipation + payoff (written BEFORE building)

1. **Genshin wish — the colour-of-the-meteor tell, fixed for the whole pull.** The streak that crosses the sky before anything is shown is blue (3★), purple (4★) or gold (5★), and "the color of the wish animation is fixed and will not change halfway through the pull" — a purple animation means no 5★ in it (same rule in Wuthering Waves: https://samurai-gamers.com/wuthering-waves/wuwa-wish-rarity-indicator-guide/; "the golden light promises a 5-star pull": https://www.highgroundgaming.com/genshin-impact-wishing-guide-pity/). MECHANISM: one pre-reveal signal, colour-coded to the BEST thing in the pull, shown BEFORE the item, and honest (never a fake-out). The tension is "is it gold?", answered a beat before "what is it?". → ours: a rarity-coloured TELL on the pointer/frame in the last ~700 ms of the spin for EPIC+ (we already dim; the dim is grey and says only "something"; it must say WHICH colour).
2. **Overwatch (Kotaku, Michael Heiberg): an EARLY rarity hint "killed your anticipation of the box's contents", so they moved the hint LATER; the box "emits a glow from its cracks before you open it"; items "rain out only at the end" (https://kotaku.com/why-opening-loot-boxes-feels-like-christmas-according-1793446800).** MECHANISM: the tell sits right before the reveal, never at the start; the pre-open object itself leaks light. → ours: the tell starts only once the reel enters its last cells (the existing DIM_CELLS gate), never at the hold; the HOLD charge leaks light from the button/plate (it charges), but that light is rarity-blind (the result is not drawn yet — honest).
3. **Duelyst / Halo 5 (same Kotaku piece): the crate "shakes" before it blows open ("a lightly-shaking silver spoon"), each revealed item gets its own "subtle chime"; Halo keeps batch packs "plain and quick" because people open many in a row.** MECHANISM: a shake RAMP before the burst; one small sound per item; and the common case must stay fast. → ours: the hold's rattle ramps (light → hard, like hold-to-buy), the release IS the burst; COMMON stays instant-ish (the skip setting), only EPIC+ earns the long sequence.
4. **Hearthstone pack (thatgamesux.com): cards land face-down in a circle; HOVERING a card back makes it "glow according to its rarity" so you can "preview how lucky the pack is before the player reveals anything"; flipping "comes with an explosion", and "as rarity increases, so does the quality of the explosion" — legendaries are "sure heavy" (https://www.thatgamesux.com/hearthstone-new-card-smell).** MECHANISM: the player's own input paces the reveal (hover/tap, one card = one beat); the payoff effect scales with rarity (same shape, more mass). → ours: TAP TO KEEP stays as the player-paced beat; the land effects already scale (shake px, burst count) — add the heavier legendary beat: shard burst from the card + a bigger sting + the card lands with a thud (scale-in) before its shine.
5. **Brawl Stars Starr Drop: "you have to tap on the screen numerous times"; "each time you tap, you gain a small chance to increase the rarity"; "you may start with a Rare Drop, and tap once to end up with a Legendary" (https://clutchpoints.com/gaming/brawl-stars-starr-drops-explained; tiers Rare 50 / Super Rare 28 / Epic 15 / Mythic 5 / Legendary 2 %: https://www.sportskeeda.com/esports/starr-drop-rates-brawl-stars-explained).** MECHANISM: tactile input (repeated taps) that cracks the shell, with rarity ESCALATION shown as the drop changing colour tier by tier — the player feels they are doing the opening. We must NOT copy the upgrade-per-tap (that is a new mechanic and would lie about our odds) — but the TACTILE part transfers: the roll is paid for by a HOLD that physically charges the reel, so the spin is something the hand did, not a menu press.
6. **Clash Royale chest (gamedeveloper.com "7 reasons"): the chest is the positive start of every session, "filled with cards that I need and want as well as cards which I don't need" — the reveal is tap-to-advance with each card its own beat, the legendary card in a chest gets its own full-screen entrance (https://gamedeveloper.com/design/7-reasons-why-you-can-t-stop-playing-clash-royale).** MECHANISM: variable reward where even the "miss" pays something (dupes count toward levels — ours: ×N toward INDEX ★); per-card cadence. → ours: the result LINE for COMMON/RARE must still pay a visible beat (name pops, ×N ticks), not vanish into the strip.
7. **CGMagazine "How games build tension": "sound effects often rise in pitch just before the reveal"; "slight delays before major events, such as charging sequences, provide time for the player's imagination to fill the gap"; "dimming lights, and screen vibration as signals that a turning point is near" (https://www.cgmagonline.com/articles/how-games-to-build-player-tension/).** MECHANISM: a rising pitch + a charge delay + dim + vibration = the pre-payoff grammar. → ours: the hold gets a rising charge tone (pitch climbs with the hold), the EPIC+ swell already rises; the legendary tell adds a low rumble under the colour.
8. (From play, not a citation) **Balatro booster packs**: cards slide in one at a time with a tick, the Legendary "Soul" card is the only card in the game whose face has a second floating layer (it wobbles above the frame) — a special that is visible at a glance, in the frame language, not by a label. **Pet Sim 99 eggs**: the egg wobbles harder the longer it is held open, cracks, and the pet pops out with a rarity-colour burst; a HUGE gets a server-wide banner. MECHANISM: the rare card is drawn DIFFERENTLY (an extra layer), not just recoloured. → ours (step 2): LEGENDARY+ frames get a second layer (the corner gems / a crown plate) the lower tiers do not have.

**CARD CRAFT (what the games' cards share, for step 2):** a thick rarity-coloured FRAME with a darker-shade outline (Clash Royale: the frame colour IS the rarity, the name sits on a plate at the bottom, the art is the focal with a backing shape; Hearthstone: a rarity GEM under the art, the name on a banner across the middle; Balatro: a plain cream frame, the rarity shown by the edition sheen; Pet Sim: the rarity is a coloured label band + a glow). The common elements: (1) frame = rarity colour, outlined in a darker shade; (2) a NAME PLATE (banner) that is its own shape, not a text line; (3) a RARITY BAND / gem, small; (4) the art FOCAL has a backing shape (circle/starburst) so the glyph never floats on the fill; (5) the hard shadow is under the whole card, never per element. Ours today: the header bar is a flat rectangle, the name is a text line on the fill, the stat band is a flat black rectangle, the foot is a 45 % black rectangle — all CSS rectangles; the cog is the only asset.
