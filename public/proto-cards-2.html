MODE CARD DIRECTIONS

PROTOTYPE · proto/cards-2

Four genuinely different ideas about what a mode card is — not four skins of the current layout. All five modes rendered in each so they read as a set. Hover to feel the lift; locked states (CHAIN / FUSE) stay legible. Real authored SVG per mode.

1 · ART-LED 2 · POSTER 3 · ARCADE CABINET 4 · DATA-FORWARD
01
ART-LED
The artwork is the whole card, full-bleed. Title, tag and badge sit on an ink scrim at the foot. The scene does the selling; type just names it.
02
POSTER
Heavy blocky type fills the card, gig-poster style. The mode name is the hero; the art is a tilted accent chip in the corner. Loud from across the room.
03
ARCADE CABINET
Each mode is its own machine — lit marquee up top, a scan-lined screen playing the mode, a control panel with the badge below. The grid reads as a row of cabinets.
04
DATA-FORWARD
The card is your record in that mode — best, mastery, wins/word, games played — so it changes as you play. Locked modes blur the numbers behind the gate.
All art is authored inline SVG (flat fills, thick outlines, hard offset shadows). Motion is transform/opacity only — hover lift + press, no idle loops. Sample stats in Direction 4 are illustrative.
